# 02_raw: the source archive

This folder holds every document, web page and data response collected for the Bangladesh Rice Variety Register, exactly as
downloaded. Nothing in it is edited after download. Each file has a row in `manifest.csv` with the address it came from,
an Internet Archive copy where one exists, the download date, the collecting agent and its SHA-256 checksum, and a row in
`CATALOGUE.csv` that says what the file is (title, issuing body, document date, document type, language, pages), whether the
register cites it, which register fields it supports, and how it may be redistributed. The catalogue is generated by
`scripts/build_catalogue.py`; the fixity of every file against the manifest is checked by `scripts/check_fixity.py`.

File paths are the keys that tie the register to its sources: the `local_file` column of every released table points to a
path in this folder. For that reason files are never moved or renamed, even where the folder names follow different logics.
Folder names reflect the order in which the archive was built: some are named after the issuing body (`sca`, `brri`), some
after the web host (`fri`, `fisheries`), some after the capture method (`moa_wayback`, `literature_api`) and some after the
workstream that collected them (`fish_governance`, `cgiar_specific`). The same body can therefore appear in more than one
folder; the catalogue's `issuing_body` column is the reliable way to find all of its documents.

## Folders

**Official records of the seed system.** `sca` holds the Seed Certification Agency's publications: the National Seed Board
minutes (`nsb_minutes_*.pdf`, the primary record of every release and registration), the SCA's annual lists of approved
inbred and registered hybrid varieties, and the 2016 compendium of approved varieties of notified crops. `sca_wayback` holds
Technical Committee minutes recovered from Internet Archive copies of the retired SCA portal. `sca_laws` holds the Seeds
Ordinance 1977 and its amendments, the Seed Act 2018, the Seed Rules 2020 and the National Seed Policy as published by the SCA.
`moa` holds Ministry of Agriculture documents taken from the national portal (the 2026 hybrid rice list, release gazettes of
2007, Seed Rules amendments), and `moa_wayback` holds Ministry documents recovered from Internet Archive copies of the retired
Ministry portal (gazettes of NSB releases 2009-2019, the 1985 variety registration notices and the evaluation and release
procedures). Two files in `moa_wayback` are truncated captures that do not open (`gazette_nsb70-83_2015-01-22.pdf`,
`gazette_nsb91-92_2017-04-27.pdf`); the manifest marks them and the register does not cite them.

**The Bangladesh Gazette.** `bgpress` holds extraordinary issues of the Bangladesh Gazette from the Government Press
(`dpp.gov.bd`) that notify rice variety releases and seed rules; `gazette_weekly` holds the weekly Part I and Part VI issues
searched for release notices. The file names carry the issue date and the Government Press item numbers.

**Laws.** `bdlaws` holds the fish acts as published on the Laws of Bangladesh site of the Legislative and Parliamentary
Affairs Division, and `faolex` holds FAOLEX copies of the Seeds Ordinance and a fish products act.

**Research institutes.** `brri` holds Bangladesh Rice Research Institute publications: variety booklets and factsheets
(`brri/factsheets`), the *Adhunik Dhaner Chash* handbooks and the adoption database extract. `brri_riceprofile` holds the
pages of the BRRI Rice Profile site, one page per variety plus the list pages. `bina` holds the Bangladesh Institute of
Nuclear Agriculture variety pages (`bina/varieties`), its variety index and annual report. `bfri` and `fri` both hold
pages and reports of the Bangladesh Fisheries Research Institute: `bfri` the annual reports, technology lists and a crawl of
the institute's site (`bfri/pages`, with machine-generated names; the catalogue gives each page's own title), `fri` its news
search pages. `gau` holds a Gazipur Agricultural University research summary and `ais` the Agriculture Information Service's
*Krishi Diary* 2026.

**Fisheries governance.** `fish_governance` holds the material of the fish-governance search: Government Press gazettes of
the Ministry of Fisheries and Livestock (`bgpress`, `bgpress_weekly`), Department of Fisheries notices and circulars (`dof`,
`fisheries`), Ministry notices (`mofl`), a customs copy of the 1985 fish rules, FAO background documents and news reports.
The older top-level folders `dof`, `fisheries` and `mofl` hold earlier downloads from the same bodies (the hatchery act and
rules, the vannamei guideline, the 2021 Subarna rui naming notification and notice search pages).

**CGIAR and partners.** `cgiar_specific` holds the seven CGIAR sources named by the principal investigator, each in its own
subfolder with the item record, its metadata and the document (GloMIP product catalogue, ASTI data, CGSpace items, an ISPC
brief). `cgiar_web` holds CGIAR web pages and CGSpace records found by search (IRRI news and *Rice Today*, STRASA,
HarvestPlus, WorldFish, AfricaRice). `cgiar_lit` holds CGIAR impact studies and their supplementary files. `worldfish`,
`harvestplus`, `asti_trivsa`, `spia_cgspace` and `fishinnovationlab` hold further documents and data from these centres and
programmes. `iaea` holds the IAEA Mutant Variety Database responses and an IAEA Bulletin article, and `fnca` an FNCA report
on mutation breeding in Bangladesh.

**Literature.** `literature_api` holds the raw responses of the literature searches (OpenAlex, Crossref, CGSpace and Europe
PMC), one JSON file per query page; it is the largest folder (about 520 MB) and only 11 of its files are cited by the
register. `literature_pages` and `literature` hold article landing pages, and `dataverse` two Harvard Dataverse metadata
records. `news` holds two news reports cited for fish and rice events.

**Survey-linked data.** `spia_bd2025` holds files of the SPIA Bangladesh Study 2025 replication package (public GitHub
repository `CGIAR-SPIA/SPIA-Bangladesh-Study-2025`, copied from a local clone), and `spia_stocktake` the SPIA stocktake of
CGIAR-related innovations in Bangladesh (July 2023), copied from a local disk. They are used for the crosswalk to survey variety names and for local names, and are not redistributed.

**Contacts.** `institutions` holds the contact pages of developers named in the register (BAU, GAU, BRAC, ACI, University of
Rajshahi), used for the institutions table.

**Provenance of the collection itself.** `wayback_cdx` holds Internet Archive CDX listings used to find retired government
pages. `_robots` holds the `robots.txt` file of every host crawled, saved on the day of the crawl, and `_manifest_parts`
holds the per-agent manifests that were merged into `manifest.csv`; neither is a source and neither has manifest rows.

## Agent codes

The `agent` column of the manifest names the workstream that collected the file. The scripts of each workstream are
`scripts/phase0_<code>_*.py`, `scripts/phase2_<code>_*.py` or `scripts/phase3_<code>_*.py`, and its judgement calls are
in `04_working/decisions_<code>.md` (phase 2 and 3) or `00_admin/phase0_agent_brief.md` (phase 0). Phase 0 discovery:
DS (datasets and literature), RR (rice regulators and law), RB (rice breeders and extension), FS (fish), LOC (files
already on the project disk; see `00_admin/decisions_log.md`, 6 October 2026). Phase 2 pilots: P2A (official lists and gazettes), P2B (institute and
CGIAR sources for rice), P2F (fish strains). Phase 3: F3C (CGIAR literature), F3I (institute sources), F3L (inbred lists),
F3W (CGIAR web), F4 (fish governance), F6G (gazettes), L1 (literature and contacts), L2 (PI-named CGIAR sources).

## Rights and personal data

Government of Bangladesh publications, CGIAR open-access outputs and openly licensed items may be redistributed with the
archive; publisher documents and third-party web pages without an open licence are listed with address and checksum but
not redistributed. The rule for each file is in the catalogue's `rights_class` column and is explained in
`00_admin/rights_and_personal_data_policy.md`. The manifest is a working file: its OpenAlex and Crossref addresses still
carry the project's polite-pool e-mail parameter, which is removed in the catalogue and in every published copy.
