# Bangladesh Rice Variety Register

A register of the rice varieties approved for cultivation in Bangladesh from 1966 to February 2026, with the National Seed Board decision behind each approval, pedigrees, stated traits, documented links to CGIAR breeding and the codes under which each variety appears in household surveys. It also covers improved fish strains and government acts on fish species.

The site is at https://kushal-satya.github.io/bgd-rice-variety-register/. The tables are in `data/` as UTF-8 CSV files and one Excel workbook, documented in `data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`, with corrections listed in `data/CHANGELOG.md`.

Mobile numbers and personal e-mail addresses that appear in the source documents have been removed from these files. Page text is in `content/text.md`; `EDITING.md` explains how to change it. `docs/PLAYBOOK.md` describes the methods for use on other countries' registers.

Kumar, K. (2026). *Bangladesh Rice Variety Register, 1966–2026: officially approved rice varieties, with improved fish strains* (Version 1.1.1) [Data set]. Cornell University.
