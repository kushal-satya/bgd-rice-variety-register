# Notice: third-party documents and data

The Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence and the MIT licence in `LICENSE` cover the register's own tables,
documentation and code. They do not cover the source documents from which the register was built, and nothing in this
repository or its deposits relicenses those documents.

The register cites about 380 source files: Bangladesh Gazette notifications, acts and rules, minutes of the National Seed
Board and its Technical Committee, variety lists of the Seed Certification Agency and the Ministry of Agriculture, notices
of the Department of Fisheries and the Ministry of Fisheries and Livestock, publications and web pages of BRRI, BINA and
BFRI, CGIAR reports, records and web pages, journal articles, news reports, and responses of the OpenAlex, Crossref,
CGSpace and Europe PMC interfaces. Each keeps the rights its publisher gives it. The source catalogue
(`02_raw/CATALOGUE.csv` in the source deposit) records, for every file, its original address, an Internet Archive copy
where one exists, its SHA-256 checksum and a rights class.

Where the source documents are deposited, only these are included in full: official publications of the Government of
Bangladesh, reproduced unaltered for research and verification with no rights claimed by the depositor; CGIAR outputs
under CGIAR's open-access policy, with the item's own licence; items under an explicit open licence (CC BY or CC0); OpenAlex
data (CC0); and Crossref bibliographic metadata without abstracts. Publisher documents, abstracts, news reports and other
web pages without an open licence, and SPIA survey-linked files whose licence has not been verified, are not redistributed; the
deposit lists them with their addresses and checksums so that a reader can obtain the same file and verify it.

Facts taken from the sources (names, dates, meeting numbers, pedigrees, yields) and short quotations given with page
references are reproduced for scholarly verification. If you hold rights in a document and object to its inclusion,
write to the author, Kushal Kumar, Cornell University, and the document will be withdrawn and replaced by its reference.

Names and posts of officials appear as printed in public government records. Telephone numbers and e-mail addresses of
individuals are removed from every public file; institutional switchboards and office mailboxes printed on letterheads are
kept. The rule is set out in `00_admin/rights_and_personal_data_policy.md`.
