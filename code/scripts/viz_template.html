Fish: improved strains and government control

The state controls fish and shrimp species, not strains. The Ministry of Fisheries and Livestock and the Department of Fisheries decide which species hatcheries may produce (Fish Hatchery Rules 2011) and import (Fish Quarantine Rules 2024), ban species by gazette, and approved one new species (vannamei, 2023). There is no equivalent of the National Seed Board for improved strains: no strain register, no required trials and no release committee. Hybrid release needs approval of the Director General of Fisheries (Fish Hatchery Act 2010, s. 6(2)), but none is on record.

Data availability

The register and its supporting tables are distributed as UTF-8 CSV files and as one Excel workbook (bgd_variety_archive.xlsx) that opens with a README and the data dictionary (DATA_DICTIONARY.md). Every source document is archived unchanged with its SHA-256 fingerprint; original addresses and Internet Archive copies are listed in bgd_sources.csv. The scripts that rebuild every table from the archived sources are distributed with the data.
